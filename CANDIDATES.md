# GPS excuse candidates

Each numbered candidate is one line. All ten are original, first-person GPS excuses under 200 characters.

1. I followed the map into the lake. If your car can't handle a shortcut with fish in it, take that up with the manufacturer.
2. I trusted the map when it sent us through the cemetery. You're the one who asked me to avoid traffic.
3. I followed the map through the zoo. That lion enclosure saved six minutes, assuming you keep running.
4. I used the map's shortcut through the golf course. Being chased by a man with a nine iron is technically live traffic.
5. I followed the map into that cornfield. You asked for a scenic route; I can't help that the scenery is now inside the car.
6. I trusted the map's route through the wedding. You said to avoid tolls, and nobody made you catch the bouquet.
7. I followed the map onto the airport runway. You wanted to beat the traffic; I found you opponents worth racing.
8. I trusted the map about that cul-de-sac. I got you to your destination twice, if you count the same mailbox.
9. I followed the map through the car wash. I said we'd arrive in two minutes; I never promised you'd still have a roof rack.
10. I followed the map down the boat ramp. You selected the ferry route; apparently you expected someone else to supply the ferry.
